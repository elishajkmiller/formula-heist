NULLCORP, an evil science company, stole every theory and formula. Break into their lab, run, jump and jetpack through the rooms, and steal them all back.

Stretch the catapult to smash through the wall, then tap to jump and hold to fire your jetpack. Dodge zappers, spikes, saws, missiles and laser gates. Pick up blueprint crates to build vehicles: a spring stomper, a gravity inverter, a quantum entangler, a DNA helix, a Bernoulli bird and an aerial screw.

Collect electrons and scratch tickets to recover 60 real physics formulas, each as a normal, holo or secret gold card. Spend electrons on shirts, pants and eight jetpack engines.

Everything is made in code: the pixel art, the sound effects and the music, which is composed as you play and changes key as you go. No ads, no tracking, no network access.
